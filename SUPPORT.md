# Getting help

**Using the app.** The [user handbook](docs/HANDBOOK.md) walks through the
everyday flow, the power features and a troubleshooting section, with
screenshots. The [feature matrix](docs/FEATURES.md) says what exists and what
does not; [ROADMAP.md](ROADMAP.md) says what is planned and what never will be.

**A question, or an idea that is not yet a concrete request.**
[Discussions](https://github.com/Rynos-PCP/Terra-Git/discussions).

**Something is broken.** Open a
[bug report](https://github.com/Rynos-PCP/Terra-Git/issues/new?template=bug_report.yml).
The form asks for your operating system, your terra-git version, your
`git --version` and a log excerpt — please fill those in, they are what makes a
bug reproducible. The daily log lives in the app's log directory, and **⋯ → Open
log folder** in the toolbar takes you straight there; after a crash there is
also a `crash-<number>.log` next to it. Skim both before attaching them: paths
contain names and remote URLs sometimes contain account names.

**A security vulnerability.** Do not open a public issue — see
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) for the private reporting channel.

**Building it, or contributing.** [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) for the
conventions, [docs/DEVELOPMENT.md](docs/DEVELOPMENT.md) for prerequisites,
project layout, tests and the release build.

terra-git is maintained in spare time. Issues are read; a reply can take a few
days.
